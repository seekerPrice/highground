# HighGround

HighGround supports small shops before, during and after flooding. These terms distinguish observations, instructions, self-reported actions and evidence limitations.

## Language

**Shop**:
A business at a confirmed location, with its own inventory and protective-action history.
_Avoid_: Household, recipient

**Recipient**:
An owner or worker who receives a shop's alerts in their chosen language. One shop can have multiple recipients.
_Avoid_: Shop

**Flood observation**:
A source report about water or weather at an attributed place and time; it may be stale, uncertain or unrelated to the shop.
_Avoid_: Confirmed flood, authenticated scene

**Flood event**:
A geographically scoped flood concern classified as Watch, Warning or Danger from relevant observations, or explicitly labelled as a simulation.
_Avoid_: Flood forecast, guaranteed outcome

**Alert**:
A message to a recipient about a flood event. Acceptance of a message by Telegram does not establish that the recipient read it.
_Avoid_: Read receipt

**Lift-first list**:
Prioritised low or floor-level inventory items, ranked by estimated value relative to lifting effort.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed salvage

**Protection confirmation**:
A recipient's report that the identified stock was lifted or a vehicle moved; it is not independent evidence of avoided losses.
_Avoid_: Proven savings

**RM protected**:
The estimated value associated with confirmed stock-lifting actions, counted without duplicate confirmations.
_Avoid_: Actual loss prevented, insured amount

**Listed destination**:
A sourced evacuation or parking location whose designation does not establish current opening, accessibility or safety.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed safe point, safe route

**File integrity**:
Agreement between stored media bytes and the recorded upload hash. It does not authenticate the scene, capture time or location.
_Avoid_: Genuine evidence, proof of loss

**Loss estimate**:
An uncertain valuation based on the baseline inventory and post-flood evidence, presented for review.
_Avoid_: Verified claim amount, insurer-approved loss
